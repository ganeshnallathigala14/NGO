Featured Story

"One Afternoon of Teaching Can Change a Child's Path"

Priya Menon joined InAmigos as a weekend tutor two years ago. What started as a few hours helping with reading has grown into a full literacy circle of 30 children in her neighborhood. "Volunteering with InAmigos showed me how far a single afternoon of teaching can go for a child," she says. Today she trains new volunteers joining the Education for Every Child program.
